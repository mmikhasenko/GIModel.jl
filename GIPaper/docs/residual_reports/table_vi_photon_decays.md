Generated by `scripts/audit_table_vi_photon_decays.jl` on 2026-07-06.

Appendix-D mock-meson overlaps `I_i(x,y)` / `E_n^i(x,y)` evaluated on
the model's own FD wavefunctions (contact-distorted S waves, central P
waves), with the paper's fitted exponents 0.7 / 0.5 and **no new fitted

exponent, so it doubles as the pipeline normalization check.

(page 24)**: each was read directly off the printed "Predicted mu"
column of PDF page 24 (`paper/vision_ocr/page_images/page-024.png`,
printed p. 212). The raw vision-OCR column (md lines 1124-1133) was
displaced by one row against the decay labels; the printed page confirms
the earlier one-row shift-correction exactly (K*+ +0.91, K*0 -1.20,

values changed by the crop audit. (Printed labels: the u-bbar row is
B*- -> B- gamma; F_b* carries footnote f.) Open-flavor formula
coefficients are rebuilt from quark charges via mu = e_q I_q - e_qbar I_qbar.

## 2S -> chi_0 photon-momentum convention test

The two `2S -> chi_0` E1 rows sit high with q from measured 1984
masses. They carry the largest q of the 2S->chi block, so the amplitude
(`~ q^{3/2}`, from `coeff(q) * sqrt(q)`) is the most q-sensitive row and
the natural place to test the mass convention. Below, `q` is recomputed
from the MODEL (GI predicted) parent/daughter masses -- eigenvalues of
the same 2^3S_1 and central 1^3P_0 solves, no new constants -- and shown
side by side with the measured-mass variant. Everything else (E_1 moment,
coefficient) is held fixed.

| Decay | q (meas) MeV | Computed (meas) | q (model) MeV | Computed (model) | Paper |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| psi' -> chi_c0 gamma | 261.0 | +0.178 | 153.9 | +0.080 | +0.14 |
| Upsilon' -> chi_b0 gamma | 161.7 | -0.033 | 120.2 | -0.021 | -0.025 |

- `psi' -> chi_c0 gamma`: measured-mass q gives 26.9% deviation, model-mass q (parent 3.680 -> daughter 3.523 GeV) gives 42.6%. **measured masses closer.**
- `Upsilon' -> chi_b0 gamma`: measured-mass q gives 30.2% deviation, model-mass q (parent 10.004 -> daughter 9.883 GeV) gives 16.5%. **model masses closer.**

## Conventions used

- `mu/mu_N = coefficient * I_i * M_N` (Table VI lists moments in units
  of `e/2`; `M_N = 0.93827 GeV`).
- E1 amplitude = `coeff(q_GeV) * E_1^i * sqrt(alpha * q_MeV)`.
- Mock masses `M~ = <E_1> + <E_2>` from the momentum-space waves.
- Phase convention: `Phi(0) > 0` for every radial level (matches the
  annihilation-block convention).
