### Isoscalar block notes

- The relative `phi->eta` (+) vs `phi->eta'` (-) sign follows from the
  signed final-state components and the fixed negative strange-quark M1
  coefficient. No report-local state rephasing or paper-sign anchor is used.
- The six isoscalar signs and the `eta'->rho > rho->eta` ordering reproduce
  the paper. Magnitudes remain 25-36% low on the largest residuals
  (`phi->eta'` -0.422 vs -0.66, `omega->eta` +0.328 vs +0.50,
  `rho->eta` +1.021 vs +1.53). These are visible physics residuals, not
  a report-local vector, phase, or wave-representation discrepancy.
- The ordering is recovered only after composing every signed radial/flavor
  component. A comparison of the two 1nn coefficients alone is insufficient:
  the physical states also contain 2nn/ss components and use their own
  transition kernels.

- **`A2 -> pi gamma` is the paper's fit row for the 0.5 exponent** (the

  `rho -> pi gamma` for the M1 pipeline. Computed +0.569 vs the +0.55 fit

- `K*(1420) -> K gamma` (strange 1P) folds the two emitting-quark channels

## W6 paper-order residual checks

The two formerly open Table VI diagnostics were re-scored with the W6 paper-order
distorted waves (native finite-HO fixed-channel diagonalization,
the harmonized Table VII treatment). **Honest negative result: neither is a
spin-wavefunction-distortion residual, so the paper-order waves do not
resolve the deeply cancelled bottomonium sign. The isoscalar ordering is
resolved instead by composing the complete physical states.**

**(a) `Upsilon'' -> eta_b gamma` hindered sign.** Recomputed on the
paper-order waves:

| row | paper-order mu | paper |
|---|---:|---:|
| Upsilon' -> eta_b gamma (hindered) | +0.0077 | +0.007 |
| Upsilon'' -> eta_b gamma (hindered) | -0.0037 | +0.007 |

For the heavy `b bbar` sector the paper-order (finite-HO full-diag) wave
equals the FD wave to a few percent, so the `Upsilon''` value moves only
from `-0.004` to `-0.004` — the deep `I - recoil` cancellation does NOT
flip sign. The paper's `+0.007` is itself an order-of-magnitude entry; the
residual sign of this doubly-cancelled `3S -> 1S` amplitude sits below the
model's resolving power, independent of the S-wave treatment. The allowed
rows and the first hindered row (`Upsilon'`) are reproduced.

**(b) `eta <-> eta'` M1 composition.** The 1nn coefficients are `a_eta^nn / a_eta'^nn = 0.624 / 0.568 = 1.10`,
but these two numbers do not determine the transition ordering. The shared
`physical_transition_amplitude` also composes the signed 2nn and ss pieces
with their native waves. The resulting `eta'->rho` (+1.419) exceeds
`rho->eta` (+1.021), agreeing with the paper's ordering without a local
phase or vector adjustment. See `w6_ho_order_validation.md` for the
paper-order treatment and its §1b light-mass discriminator.

## Conventions used

- `mu/mu_N = coefficient * I_i * M_N` (Table VI lists moments in units
  of `e/2`; `M_N = 0.93827 GeV`).
- E1 amplitude = `coeff(q_GeV) * E_1^i * sqrt(alpha * q_MeV)`.
- Mock masses `M~ = <E_1> + <E_2>` from the momentum-space waves.
- Phase convention: `Phi(0) > 0` for every radial level (matches the
  annihilation-block convention).
