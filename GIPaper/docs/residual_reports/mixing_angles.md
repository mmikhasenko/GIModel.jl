## Convention mapping (derived once, applied uniformly)

The paper prints the same rotation matrix in all three captions:

```
[ Q_low  ]   [  cos(theta_nL)  sin(theta_nL) ] [ n ^1L_L ]
[ Q_high ] = [ -sin(theta_nL)  cos(theta_nL) ] [ n ^3L_L ]
```

so the *lower-mass* eigenstate is
`Q_low = cos(theta) |n ^1L_L> + sin(theta) |n ^3L_L>` with `cos(theta) >= 0`
implied by the quoted range.

The production spectrum diagonalizes one complete block containing every
requested radial singlet and triplet with this `L,J`. For each quoted `nL`,
we take the lower of the two physical states assigned to its singlet/triplet
precursors, project its shared eigenvector onto those two exact basis rows,
fix the singlet component positive, and report
`theta = atan2(v_triplet,nL, v_singlet,nL)`. Thus the angular convention is
identical to the paper while radial-state mixing remains present, so

> **theta_paper = theta_model (identity angular mapping; the model value is a projection of the complete radial block).**

Two residual sign conventions enter only through the off-diagonal element
`c` and are pinned once, globally:

1. **Constituent ordering.** `Meson(mq, f1, f2)` is the `f1 f2bar` meson;
   the antisymmetric spin-orbit operator carries `(1/m1^2 - 1/m2^2)/2` and
   flips sign under quark <-> antiquark exchange. Flavors are ordered
   exactly as the paper's sector labels: `u sbar`, `c ubar`, `c sbar`,
   `b ubar`, `b sbar`, `b cbar`.
2. **Basis phase.** The angular matrix element is
   `<^1L_L| L.(S1-S2) |^3L_L> = +sqrt(L(L+1))` with `S1` the quark (`f1`)
   spin (`spin_orbit_mixing_components`, `src/spin_fine_structure.jl`).

Consistency check (an outcome, not a fit): with this fixed mapping the
model reproduces the paper's sector-wide sign pattern from the mass
ordering alone — strange (`u` quark, heavy antiquark) angles positive,
charmed (heavy quark, light antiquark) angles negative.

The five largest direct-angle differences are:
- `b cbar 1P`: model +77.7 deg, paper -53 deg (Delta +130.7 deg); same-n projection leaves 0.00% norm in other radial rows.
- `u sbar 1P`: model +87.5 deg, paper +34 deg (Delta +53.5 deg); same-n projection leaves 0.02% norm in other radial rows.
- `c ubar 1P`: model -77.7 deg, paper -41 deg (Delta -36.7 deg); same-n projection leaves 0.00% norm in other radial rows.
- `c sbar 1P`: model -78.8 deg, paper -44 deg (Delta -34.8 deg); same-n projection leaves 0.00% norm in other radial rows.
- `b ubar 1P`: model -76.6 deg, paper -43 deg (Delta -33.6 deg); same-n projection leaves 0.00% norm in other radial rows.

Near-degenerate singlet/triplet diagonals remain sensitive to small matrix
changes; when the physical low/high labeling is reversed, the complementary
angle in the diagnostics is the relevant comparison. The explicit outside-
projection percentage distinguishes that convention issue from genuine
cross-radial composition in the complete block.

The antisymmetric element is itself cancellation-sensitive: its vector
color-magnetic and scalar/Thomas pieces usually have opposite signs.
The survival percentage below is `|vector + Thomas| / (|vector| + |Thomas|)`.
A small value localizes the discrepancy to the relative strength of the two
Appendix-A spin-orbit kernels; it is not evidence of radial contamination.
