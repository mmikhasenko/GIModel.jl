# Independent FD Comparator Convergence Audit

Generated by `julia GIPaper/scripts/audit_fd_convergence.jl`.
Numerics: `FiniteDifferenceSolver` — ngrid = 2400, rmax = 32.0 GeV^-1 (h = 0.01333), relativistic, full, 6 levels/channel / `OscillatorSolver` — nbasis = 24:8:80 adaptive, ΔE ≤ 0.1 MeV, beta in [0.25, 2.35] GeV (22 bracket points, refined to 0.002 GeV), 6 levels/channel.

This certifies the modern finite-difference comparator on its own terms.
It is diagnostic evidence for the package and is **not** a step or
acceptance condition of the original 1985 finite-HO algorithm.

## Protocol

The two FD error sources are varied independently:

1. **Resolution:** `ngrid` increases at fixed `rmax = 32 GeV^-1`.
2. **Extent:** `rmax` increases at fixed `h = 1/60 GeV^-1`.

The calibration covers six radial levels in equal-flavor q, s, c, and b
`1S0` and `3P2` sectors. Thus the endpoints include diffuse light states,
compact bottomonium, the resummed contact kernel, and the spin-orbit/tensor
kernels. Wave tests use RMS radii for all six levels and the signed Eq. (17)
smeared-origin functional for n = 1, 3, and 6.

Internal gates: mass 0.20 MeV; RMS radius 0.10%; smeared origin 0.50%.
Each is the largest change over the named levels between the final two
points of a sweep. HO differences are never included in these gates.

## Observable momentum cutoff

The production FD grid historically had a Nyquist momentum just below
60 GeV. FD-COMP caps finer-grid observable transforms at 60 GeV so that
coordinate refinement does not enlarge and coarsen a fixed-size momentum
quadrature. This independent cutoff sweep keeps Δp fixed and checks the
q/b endpoint flavors at n = 1, 3, and 6. The 60→80 GeV change must remain
below 0.01%.

## Full mixing pipeline

The q-s block exercises unequal-mass antisymmetric spin-orbit mixing; the
c-c block exercises the physically important P/F cross-orbital tensor
mixing directly. Composition defects are
`1 - |<c_a|c_b>|`, so an irrelevant whole-state sign cannot fake a failure.
The non-leading weight `1 - max |c_i|^2` demonstrates that the named
mechanism is active rather than passing through an unmixed state.

## Conclusion

FD is now a separately certified modern comparator over eigenvalues,
short- and long-distance wave functionals, cross-state overlaps, and the
post-sector mixing pipeline. The production default remains a fast report
setting; precision cross-checks should use the certified grid above.
No public mesh access, fallback, or additional wave/spectrum holder was
introduced. The comparison calls the same representation-independent
physics consumers as the native-HO path.
