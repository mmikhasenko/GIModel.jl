**Key conventions / findings that make Table V reproduce:**

1. **Leading-S0 convention.** The structure-dependent (S/D/P) numeric column
   uses the leading constant `S0 = 3 h beta` (dropping the `-k A qbar^2`
   polynomial of Table IV); this fixes `S0 ~ 3.29` and reproduces the D/P
   rows to ~1% (e.g. `rho -> [omega pi]_P` -7.82 vs -7.8).
2. **`K*2 -> K pi` sqrt(3) resolved.** The old provisional coefficient
   `+(3/20)^1/2` was a digitization error; the page-image canonical value is
   `+(1/20)^1/2` (sqrt(3) smaller), giving +7.60 vs paper +7.7. Not a physics
   anomaly -- a transcription bug the canonical re-digitization fixed.
3. **Same-J mixing rotation.** The Q1/Q2 (and Q1c/Q2c) rows are the physical
   mixed states: rotating the pure singlet/triplet formulas by the paper's
   angle (1P ~ +34 deg, 1D ~ +33 deg, charm ~ -41 deg) reproduces the
   footnote-j near-cancellations in sign and magnitude (`Q1->[K*pi]_S` -0.34
   vs -0.3; `Q2->[K*pi]_S` +17.4 vs +16).
4. **Residual MISSes are parent-mass sensitivity, not algebra.** Every
   structure-independent MISS inverts to a parent mass within 20-50 MeV of
   the input (D-waves' `qbar^2` amplifies it ~2x over their matched S-wave
   partners); the paper's numbers imply the GI-predicted masses
   (`a1, H ~ 1.22 GeV`). See the `[M used -> paper implies]` note per row.
