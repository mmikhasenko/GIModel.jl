## Current Finding

This does not look like a column-ordering, phase, one-line sign, coordinate/momentum `alpha_s`, Eq. (17) FD integral, or `ns` flavor-coherence bug. The exact FD momentum integral and coherent `ns` factor are now implemented in the paper P1/P2 path, but they still under-mix Table III. The remaining focused suspect is the paper-order basis entering the annihilation matrix: HO-diagonalized wavefunctions/diagonal masses and the phenomenological pseudoscalar prescription, rather than a local prefactor retune.
