Table VII Audit — Annihilation Amplitudes ​

All kinematic M and M_P values use the pinned PDG 2026 experimental registry. M̃ and S_L remain wavefunction integrals. NaN denotes an unavailable experimental assignment; no GI/model mass fallback is used. Width-reference measurements below are legacy comparisons, not updated PDG 2026 width inputs.

Numerics: OscillatorSolver — nbasis = 24:8:80 adaptive, ΔE ≤ 0.1 MeV, beta in [0.25, 2.35] GeV (22 bracket points, refined to 0.002 GeV), 6 levels/channel.

Generated by julia GIPaper/checks/audit_table_vii.jl. All 61 canonical rows in the four subtables are accounted for: 57 are computed from the model wavefunctions and four hypothetical toponium rows are explicitly unavailable. The only fitted quantity anywhere is the charge-radius smearing exponent f = 0.2 (the paper's own π⁺ fit); every other amplitude is parameter-free.

Both slices run on the Eq. (17)-style smeared momentum integral over the jₗ-transformed radial wave. Phase convention throughout: each radial wave's outermost antinode is positive, so the wavefunction-at-origin — and every amplitude below — flips sign once per radial node. The overall sign of a single width is unobservable (a rephasing of |M>); matching the paper's alternating signs under one consistent convention demonstrates the node structure is right.

Leptonic decays (part a) ​

f = coefficient · factor
  P_P   = M⁻¹ M̃^(-1/2) K[√(m₁m₂/E₁E₂)]     (¹S₀ singlet wave)
  V_V   = M⁻² M̃^(+1/2) K[√(m₁m₂/E₁E₂)]     (³S₁ triplet wave)
  V'_V  = M⁻² M̃^(+1/2) K[(m/E)(1 − m/E)]   (³D₁ central wave)
  P'_A1 = M⁻² M̃^(+1/2) K[m·p/E²]           (³P₁ central wave)
  K[w]  = (2π)^(-3/2) ∫d³p (4π)^(-1/2) Φ_L(p) w(p)

coefficient is the printed quark-charge/flavor factor of the formula column (e.g. 2√3 for f_π, (16/3)^(1/2) for f_ψ); M is the experimental PDG 2026 mass from the shared registry; M̃ is the model mock mass <E₁+E₂> over the same wave (mock_meson_mass). The tabulated amplitude is dimensionless (the weak rows are f_P/M_P: the experiment column shows f_π/M_π = 0.95).

The formula column is for unmixed/ideally-mixed states; the paper's numerical column additionally folds in Table III isoscalar mixing, which is a small effect for the near-ideally-mixed ω/φ — part of the residual here. The hypothetical t-tbar ζ row is not modelled.

Two-photon decays (part b) ​

A(P→γγ)   = √6   q_eff (α/m) (M/M̃)^(3/2) (1/2π)     ∫d³p φ_P(p) [m/E]
A(³P₂→γγ) = −√(4/5) q_eff (α/m) (M/M̃)^(3/2) (2/π)^(1/2) ∫dp p² Φ(p) [m·p/E²]

q_eff = Σ aᵢ eᵢ² is the state's effective squared charge (flavor amplitude times quark charges: (e_u²−e_d²)/√2 for π/A2, 4/9 for cc̄, 1/9 for bb̄/ss̄); α the fine-structure constant; M the meson mass, M̃ the mock mass. Amplitude² is Γ; units follow the paper (π in eV^½, the rest in keV^½).

r_E² = Σᵢ eᵢ [ <rᵢ²> + (3/4mᵢ²) ∫d³p |φ(p)|² (mᵢ/Eᵢ)^{2f} ]

Decay widths (Eqs. D7-D9) ​

Γ(P→ℓν)   = G² f_P² m_ℓ² / (8π M_P) · (M_P² − m_ℓ²)²        (D7)
Γ(V→ℓ⁺ℓ⁻) = (4π/3) α² M_V f_V²                              (D8)
