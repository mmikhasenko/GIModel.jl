Equations to code ​

This page maps each equation of Godfrey and Isgur (1985) to the code that implements it. Names in code font without a link are internal functions; they are listed so that you can find the implementation, not as supported API. Paths are relative to the repository root.

Every spin-dependent operator is built once and used both to assemble the Hamiltonian and to report its contribution, so the reported pieces always add up to the mass. The finite-difference and oscillator solvers share the angular and mass algebra; they differ only in how radial matrices are represented.

Details that matter ​

  • The contact density is the Laplacian of the smeared Coulomb potential,  , not  .

  • The tensor operator uses the Pauli , so the Appendix A spin bracket carries a factor .

  • Eq. (A16) contains only the confinement derivatives of the and pairs; the Coulomb derivative enters through (A15) alone.

  • The contact term acts in every partial wave. Smearing gives it finite range, and Eqs. (23)–(26) of the paper keep it in P waves.

Central-potential variants ​

CentralPotentialMethod selects how the spin-independent potential is built. Only one of them is the paper's; the rest exist to measure how much each ingredient matters:

methodconstruction
AppendixAMomentumSandwichclosed-form and with : the paper's prescription, used by the shipped parameters
AppendixAClosedFormclosed-form   without the momentum factor
PointwiseCentralunsmeared  
Coulomb1DSmearing, AppendixASmearing3D, AppendixADerivativeGdiagnostic smearing approximations (mesh only)

central_potential_path reports which one a parameter set uses.
